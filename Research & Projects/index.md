## Research & Projects

### Fast Quantitative MRI Imaging  
**Project Leader | Undergraduate Innovation Program**  
*Sep 2024 – Present*

- Designed a deep learning–based framework for fast quantitative MRI reconstruction.
- Implemented models using **PyTorch**, integrating **U-Net**, histogram matching, and **GAN-based** methods.
- Enhanced image contrast and spatial resolution under different **T1 relaxation times**.
- Aimed to reduce MRI acquisition time while preserving diagnostic image quality.
- Completed project proposal and mid-term report; actively participated in group meetings and technical discussions.

### Motion Artifact Detection in Brain MRI  
**Course Project – Medical Image Processing**  
*Jan 2023 – Jun 2023*

- Built **binary and three-class classification models** for MRI motion artifact detection.
- Employed **ResNet-18** architecture with residual connections to improve deep network training.
- Performed data preprocessing and **five-fold cross-validation**.
- Achieved **87% accuracy** in binary classification for motion artifact detection.
